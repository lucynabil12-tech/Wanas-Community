## 📌 Project Overview
**Wanas Community** is a specialized marketing and campaign agency designed to support community organizers and companies that host genuine human interaction and offline relationship-building experiences. We help youth move beyond screen isolation by promoting direct, activity-based socialization.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
* **Promote Offline Interactions:** Support event organizers in encouraging real-world social engagement.
* **Targeted Campaigns:** Execute data-driven digital marketing strategies for local community events.
* **Reduce Digital Isolation:** Help the youth connect through activity-focused social gatherings.

---

## 🛠️ Project Scope
* **Marketing Strategy & Branding:** Developing campaign themes and visual identity for social events.
* **Community Outreach:** Connecting event organizers with their target audience through digital channels.
* **Analytics & Performance:** Tracking campaign conversion rates and engagement metrics.

---

## 📅 Project Plan (5-Week Roadmap)

| Week | Phase | Key Deliverables & Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Week 2** | Media & Content Creation | Producing promotional visuals, event teasers, and branding materials. |

| **Week 5** | Final Review & Wrap-up | Finalizing documentation, GitHub repository setup, and final project presentation. |
